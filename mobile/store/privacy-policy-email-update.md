# Privacy policy update: verification and password-reset email

Paste these into the CipherRush section (12) of https://aximoix.com/privacy/,
and change "Last updated" at the top. The site's source isn't in this repo.

## 12.1 What CipherRush collects - replace the "Your account" bullet

> **Your account:** your username and email address. We use your email address
> only to run your account: to confirm it's yours when you sign up, to send
> password-reset links you ask for, and to reply if you contact us. We never
> send marketing to it. If you create a password, we store only a secure one-way
> hash of it (bcrypt). We never see or store the password itself. Confirmation
> codes and reset links are also stored only as one-way hashes, and expire
> (codes after 30 minutes, reset links after 15 minutes, and each reset link
> works once).

## 12.2 What other players can see - add at the end

> Accounts that haven't confirmed their email address don't appear on the
> leaderboards until they do.

## 12.3 How we use it - add a bullet after the first one

> To email you a code to confirm your address when you sign up, and a link to
> reset your password when you ask for one

## 12.4 Who it's shared with - replace the paragraph

> Only with the service providers that host and run CipherRush's servers and
> database (Render and Cloudflare), with Zoho ZeptoMail, which delivers our
> confirmation and password-reset emails (it receives your email address,
> username and the email's contents), with Google or Apple when you choose to
> sign in with them, and with authorities when the law requires it, as
> described in section 4. These providers may only use your information to
> provide their service to us.

## Section 4 - one fix

Section 4 lists "our email delivery service (Resend)". For CipherRush the
email provider is Zoho ZeptoMail. Either add it ("Resend and Zoho ZeptoMail")
or note it's covered in section 12.4.

# Privacy policy update: ads in the apps (Google AdMob)

Paste these too, in the same section 12. The web version has no ads; these
apply to the iOS and Android apps.

## 12.1 What CipherRush collects - add a bullet

> **Ads (apps only):** the iOS and Android apps show ads from Google AdMob.
> To serve them, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's ad software on your
> device receives your IP address (from which it estimates a rough location),
> device and app information, how you interact with the ads, and your
> device's advertising identifier (on iOS, only if you've allowed it; we never
> ask for tracking permission). Every ad we request is non-personalised: it
> isn't chosen from a profile of your interests.

## 12.4 Who it's shared with - add after the paragraph

> In the apps, the ad information described in 12.1 goes to Google, which uses
> it under its own privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and
> explains how at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
> When you watch a video for a reward, Google also tells our server that the
> video was watched, with your account ID, so we can grant the reward. We
> don't give Google your email address or your username.

## 12.x Your choices - add

> In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before
> showing ads, and you can change your choice any time in Profile → Account →
> Ad privacy choices. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID
> in your device's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads). On
> iOS, ads aren't linked to your advertising identifier unless you've allowed
> tracking.

## Remove

Any line saying CipherRush has no ads or no trackers.
