# Privacy policy update: verification and password-reset email

Paste these into the CipherRush section (12) of https://aximoix.com/privacy/,
and change "Last updated" at the top. The site's source isn't in this repo.

## 12.1 What CipherRush collects - replace the "Your account" bullet

> **Your account:** your username and email address. We use your email address
> only to run your account: to confirm it's yours when you sign up, to send
> password-reset links you ask for, and to reply if you contact us. We never
> send marketing to it. If you create a password, we store only a secure one-way
> hash of it (bcrypt). We never see or store the password itself. Confirmation
> codes and reset links are also stored only as one-way hashes, and expire
> (codes after 30 minutes, reset links after 15 minutes, and each reset link
> works once).

## 12.2 What other players can see - add at the end

> Accounts that haven't confirmed their email address don't appear on the
> leaderboards until they do.

## 12.3 How we use it - add a bullet after the first one

> To email you a code to confirm your address when you sign up, and a link to
> reset your password when you ask for one

## 12.4 Who it's shared with - replace the paragraph

> Only with the service providers that host and run CipherRush's servers and
> database (Render and Cloudflare), with Zoho ZeptoMail, which delivers our
> confirmation and password-reset emails (it receives your email address,
> username and the email's contents), with Google or Apple when you choose to
> sign in with them, and with authorities when the law requires it, as
> described in section 4. These providers may only use your information to
> provide their service to us.
